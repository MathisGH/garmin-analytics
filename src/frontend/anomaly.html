How to read this page
  • Two models look at each day of my watch data (heart rate, stress, body battery, respiration) and ask: "does this day look like my usual days?"
  • IF (Isolation Forest) works on 16 daily statistics (mean, std, min, max of each metric). AE (Autoencoder) works on the full 5-minute curves.
  • Scores have no unit and no medical meaning on their own. A day is flagged by comparing it to all my other days:

Normal among the 85 % most typical days   Unusual among the 15 % most extreme days   Very unusual among the 5 % most extreme days

By construction, about 15 % of days are flagged whatever happens, so "unusual" means "different from my own habits", not "unhealthy". The two models do not always agree: that is expected on such a small dataset.
